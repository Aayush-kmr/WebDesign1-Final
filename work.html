Selected projects.

A focused set of projects showing how I approach autonomy, sensing, visualization, and interactive systems.

01 · Autonomous systems

INARA

INARA, or Intelligent Navigation & Autonomous Response Agent, explores low-cost indoor autonomy. The work starts in a digital twin, compares learned navigation against a classical planner, and later transfers the system to a small rover.

I lead the system architecture, ROS 2 workflow, reinforcement-learning experiments, safety fallback logic, hardware planning, and evaluation strategy. The project received a $5,000 Bearcat AI Grant through UC XR Labs.

Problem
Safe, adaptable indoor robot navigation
Tools
ROS 2, Gazebo, Nav2, PPO/SAC, LiDAR
Role
System design, research, and integration
Status
Active research and prototype development
02 · Digital twin

UC Digital Futures Active Digital Twin

At XR Labs, I worked on a live digital twin of UC's Digital Futures building. The aim was a spatial environment that connects BIM geometry, sensor data, lighting controls, and immersive interfaces.

I contributed to data workflows, visualization, and XR interaction, including live lighting values, two-way control concepts, Omniverse scenes, and experiments with HoloLens and browser streaming.

Problem
Make building data spatial and interactive
Tools
Omniverse, Unreal, BIM, WebSockets, XR
Role
XR research fellow and technical prototyper
Focus
Real-time visualization and interaction
03 · Computer vision

Human Tracking & Spatial Analytics

This prototype detects people in a camera feed and remaps each detection into a top-down plan using a calibrated homography. It maintains track IDs, logs coordinates, counts occupants, and can create heatmap data.

I built it around a FastAPI backend, OpenCV processing, YOLO-based detection, and a real-time browser interface.

Problem
Turn camera detections into spatial data
Tools
Python, OpenCV, YOLO, FastAPI, WebSocket
Role
Full prototype development
Output
Positions, IDs, counts, and CSV logs
04 · Immersive training

VR Decision Training Simulation

I helped develop an interactive VR training experience built around branching decisions. The scenario changes based on the choice a user makes inside the headset.

My work focused on Unreal Engine interaction flow, scoring logic, level transitions, dialogue timing, and making the experience understandable in VR.

Problem
Practice high-stakes decisions safely
Tools
Unreal Engine 5, Blueprints, Meta Quest
Role
Interaction logic and implementation
Format
Branching immersive simulation
